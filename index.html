The showroom

A room you can
walk into.

Gulf House Furniture is a furnishing showroom on Al Rashidiya 2 in Ajman — and everything inside is set out the way it would live in a home.

Carved and gilded majlis sets upholstered in damask and jacquard. Deep, rolled-arm sofas and wing chairs in cream, sand and soft grey. Marble-topped centre and side tables. Floor-to-ceiling drapery — sheers behind, heavy drapes in front, tasselled swags and pelmets above. Crystal and gilt chandeliers overhead.

Nothing is stacked against a wall. You see how a frame catches the light, how a fabric sits beside the curtain next to it, and how the whole room reads — before any of it reaches your home.

Come in, sit down, take your time.

Collections

What sits on the floor.

Four things the showroom is built around — and they are meant to be chosen together.

01

Majlis & Salon Sets

Hand-carved frames in gilt and ivory, deep-buttoned bolsters, damask and jacquard upholstery — with the matching centre and side tables to finish the arrangement.

02

Sofas & Armchairs

Rolled-arm sofas, wing chairs and generous two-seaters in cream, sand and soft grey — the quiet pieces that hold a room together.

03

Curtains & Drapery

Layered sheers and heavy drapes, tasselled swags, pelmets, tie-backs and finials — the difference between a furnished room and a finished one.

04

Chandeliers & Lighting

Crystal and gilt-brass chandeliers sized to the ceiling they hang from — chosen last, and the first thing anyone notices.

A majlis is where a home receives its guests. It deserves to be chosen in person.
